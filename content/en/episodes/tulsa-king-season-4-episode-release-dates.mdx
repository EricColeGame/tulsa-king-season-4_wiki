### The simple formula for guessing a finale date

Once a premiere date is official, you can estimate the rest of Tulsa King Season 4 episode release dates with basic arithmetic:

1. Note the premiere date and the announced episode count.
2. Subtract one week from the total — episode one already aired on premiere night.
3. Add that many weeks to the premiere date to reach the projected finale.
4. Adjust by one week if the premiere is a double drop, or if a holiday preemption is announced.

If a ten-episode season premiered on a Sunday, episode ten would land nine weeks later on a Tuesday or Sunday, depending on the slot the network chooses. Community reports suggest fans usually pin the finale date within a week of the actual airing using this method.
