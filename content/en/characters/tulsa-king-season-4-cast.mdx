## The Core Crew: Who's Still Riding With Dwight

### Sylvester Stallone as Dwight Manfredi

Everything in *Tulsa King* radiates outward from Dwight. A New York capo who served 25 years and got shipped to Oklahoma as a consolation prize, he turned a dispensary owner, a driver, and a bar owner into a functioning crew. The role was Stallone's first leading part in a scripted TV series, and by Season 4 it's hard to imagine anyone else delivering those deadpan monologues.

### The Tulsa Originals: Bodhi, Tyson, and Mitch

These three are the spine of the show's found-family dynamic, and all three are confirmed for the new season.

- **Martin Starr as Bodhi** — the dispensary owner whose reluctant partnership with Dwight became genuine loyalty.
- **Jay Will as Tyson** — Dwight's driver and most ambitious protégé, the character most likely to inherit the operation someday.
- **Garrett Hedlund as Mitch** — the ex-con bar owner who translates Dwight's old-school mob logic into something that works in Oklahoma.

### The Family and the New York Thread

Dwight's personal life remains part of the engine. **Annabella Sciorra** returns as Joanne, Dwight's younger sister and his strongest link to the family he lost behind bars. **Dana Delany** is back as Margaret Devereaux, owner of the Fennario Ranch and Dwight's most complicated personal relationship. **Chris Caldovino** returns as Goodie Carangi, the Invernizzi family consigliere who has become steadily more important to the Tulsa side of the story — a recurring player promoted to series regular in Season 3.
